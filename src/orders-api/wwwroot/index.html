Food highlights

Easy Stockholm eats between sessions

Morning

Cinnamon bun and coffee

Start with a classic fika near the venue or in a quiet old-town café.

Lunch

Open-faced sandwiches

Smörgås, salmon, shrimp, and seasonal salads are easy, fast, and very Stockholm.

Evening

Waterfront dinner

Book a table along the quays for northern seafood, local beer, and sunset views.
