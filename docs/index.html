About

Community-driven Agentic AI knowledge

Inspired by Azure Back to School, Azure Advent Calendar, and Azure Spring Clean — free, quarterly, and 100% community-powered.

Mission

Why Agentic AI Insights?

In the spirit of the #AzureFamily community — the same energy that powers Azure Advent Calendar, Azure Spring Clean, and Azure Back to School — Agentic AI Insights dedicates a whole quarter to one of the most transformative shifts in AI: autonomous, goal-driven agents.

Every three months, practitioners, MVPs, and enthusiasts from across the community come together to publish blog posts, record short videos, ship demos, and release open-source tools — all focused on real-world Agentic AI.

Contributors share their work on social media using #AgenticAIInsights and link back to this site, growing a permanent, freely accessible knowledge base for everyone.

How It Works

📢 Call for Contributions Opens (Week 1)

At the start of each quarter, we open submissions via a simple form. Blog posts, videos, demos, and open-source projects are all welcome.

✍️ Community Creates Content (Weeks 2–10)

Contributors publish their content on their own platforms — personal blogs, YouTube, GitHub — and submit the link. We link everything from this site.

📅 Edition Goes Live (Week 11)

The full edition schedule is published here, just like Azure Back to School — a table of dates, topics, and contributors.

🎉 Community Celebration & Wrap-up (Week 13)

We round up the edition with a community call, highlights post, and open nominations for the next quarter's theme.

Contribute

Share your knowledge

Every quarter we welcome new contributions. No prior experience needed — first-time contributors are warmly encouraged.

Process

How to contribute

1

Pick a topic

Choose something you've built, learned, or experimented with in Agentic AI. Real experience beats theory every time.

2

Choose your format

Blog post, short video, live demo, or open-source project — pick whatever suits your topic and style.

3

Publish on your own platform

Host your content wherever you like — your blog, YouTube, GitHub, or a talk recording. We'll link to it from this site.

4

Submit the form

Fill in the form with your name, content type, title, and link. We'll review and add it to the edition schedule.

5

Share it with the community

Post on LinkedIn and X/Twitter with #AgenticAIInsights and a link back to this site.

Guidelines

Contribution guidelines

✔

Content must be focused on Agentic AI — agents, frameworks, tools, patterns, or real-world use cases.

✔

Practical and hands-on content is preferred over high-level overviews.

✔

Content must be original and published by you. No reposts of others' work.

✔

No product marketing or sponsored content. Community-first, always.

✔

All skill levels welcome — beginner walk-throughs are just as valuable as advanced architecture posts.
